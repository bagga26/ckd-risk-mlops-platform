Clinical Protocol & Design Principles

This platform serves as a non-diagnostic Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) developed to address delayed detection of Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) in primary care environments.

By leveraging TreeSHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), the model provides local biomarker attributions for every individual inference, eliminating "black-box" predictions and allowing clinicians to assess which lab parameters contributed most to the risk profile.

Strict Regulatory & Medical Boundary: This system does not diagnose pathology or prescribe medical intervention. Risk probabilities and tiered classifications are intended strictly for clinical prioritization and triaging. Final diagnostic determination requires clinical validation by a licensed medical practitioner.

Dataset Provenance & Technical Stack

Benchmark Dataset: University of California Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository — Chronic Kidney Disease Dataset (400 cases, 24 clinical features).

Technology Architecture: Scikit-Learn (Pipelines, Imputation, Random Forest), SHAP (TreeExplainer), FastAPI (Asynchronous REST API, Pydantic Boundary Validation), Docker (Multi-stage Containerization), and Native JavaScript/HTML5 UI.
