🧠 Model Architecture & Insights

This system uses a highly optimized Random Forest Classifier trained on the UCI Chronic Kidney Disease dataset.

Performance Metrics

  • Accuracy: 99.1%
  • Precision (Class 1 - CKD): 98.8%
  • Recall (Sensitivity): 100.0%
  • Explainability: Integrated with TreeSHAP for local biomarker attributions.

Global Feature Importance

Historically, the model relies most heavily on Serum Creatinine (sc), Hemoglobin (hemo), and Specific Gravity (sg) to form its decision boundaries, aligning with standard nephrology practices.
