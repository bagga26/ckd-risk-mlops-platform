Global Explainability & Feature Hierarchy

Trained on the UCI CKD benchmark dataset using Random Forest with TreeSHAP attribution.

Rank Biomarker / Feature Clinical Significance Global SHAP Influence
#1 Serum Creatinine (sc) Primary indicator of glomerular filtration rate (GFR). Elevated levels signal declining renal clearance. Primary Risk Driver
#2 Hemoglobin (hemo) Decreased levels indicate anemia caused by diminished renal erythropoietin secretion. Strong Protective Factor (when normal)
#3 Specific Gravity (sg) Assesses renal tubular ability to concentrate urine. Low fixed gravity implies tubular injury. High Discriminative Power
#4 Albumin (al) Persistent micro/macroalbuminuria reflects breakdown of glomerular filtration barrier. Direct Risk Driver
#5 Blood Glucose Random (bgr) Screens for diabetic nephropathy, one of the leading underlying etiologies for CKD. Moderate Risk Driver
